# Rule years and surcharge cap

The fixture publishes INR 80 per USD on 31 December 2022, the previous month-end for the buy. Each tax year contains one sale and recognizes INR 8,000 gain. Base tax is INR 1,000. A 37% elected surcharge caps at 15%, or INR 150. Cess is 4% × INR 1,150 = INR 46, for INR 1,196 total. The reports carry the matching Acts, currency conversion labels, and Schedule FA calendar years.
